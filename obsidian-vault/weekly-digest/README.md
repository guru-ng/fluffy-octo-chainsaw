# Weekly Digests

This folder holds weekly summaries of what you built, shipped, or thought about — raw material for turning into blog posts.

## Format (suggested)

Name files `YYYY-WNN.md` (e.g. `2025-W24.md`) so they sort chronologically.

```markdown
# Week 24 — 9–15 Jun 2025

## Shipped
- 

## In progress
- 

## Interesting problems / decisions
- 

## Ideas this sparked
- 
```

## How to use with Claude

1. Fill in the digest at the end of your week (5–10 minutes).
2. When you want to turn something into a post, share the relevant digest entry with Claude and say: _"Draft a blog post from the 'Interesting problems' section. Follow my style guide."_
3. Claude will produce a draft with correct frontmatter, ready to drop into `src/content/post/`.

Digests don't need to be polished — bullet points and rough notes are fine. The rougher the input, the more your voice comes through in the final post.
